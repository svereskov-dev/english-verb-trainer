UI Refinements — Practice Screen

There are two small UI improvements needed on the Practice screen.

1. Increase spacing between the verb and its IPA transcription

Currently, the IPA transcription is positioned too close to the verb.

Example:

break
/breɪk/
ломать

This makes the content feel slightly cramped.

Required change

Increase the vertical spacing between:

the verb;
the IPA transcription.

Only a small increase is needed (approximately 6–10 px).

The spacing between the IPA transcription and the translation can remain unchanged.

Do not modify typography, font sizes, or colors.

The goal is simply to improve visual readability.

2. Keep the "English Tenses" button perfectly centered

On desktop the English Tenses button appears centered.

However, on mobile devices it shifts left or right depending on the width of the neighboring controls (especially the Training Mode selector).

This should never happen.

Required behavior

The English Tenses button must always remain perfectly centered horizontally within the screen, regardless of:

the width of the Training Mode selector;
localization or text length;
screen size;
orientation.

The left Training Mode selector and the right Correct/Incorrect counters should not influence the button's position.

The layout should behave like this:

Left: Training Mode selector
Center: English Tenses button (always mathematically centered)
Right: Correct / Incorrect counters

The center button must stay fixed in the center even if the elements on the left or right change their width.

Use an appropriate layout solution (for example, absolute positioning or a three-column layout with an independent centered element) rather than relying on flexible spacing.

Expected result

The top bar should look balanced and consistent on every device:

Training Mode selector aligned left;
English Tenses button always perfectly centered;
Correct/Incorrect counters aligned right.

No element should push the center button away from the middle of the screen.
