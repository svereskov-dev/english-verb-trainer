Audit approved. Please proceed with implementation, with the following clarifications and final requirements.

1. Free content

The only tense content that is guaranteed to be free is:

Present Simple
Past Simple
Future Simple

These three tenses must remain fully usable with all free training mechanics.

Also remain free:

regular Practice mode
Context sentences
Letter Builder
Mistakes Review
statistics
progress
SRS
guides and other currently free educational content

We are monetizing additional grammar content, not training mechanics.

2. Premium content

Full Access unlocks:

Present Continuous
Past Continuous
Future Continuous
Present Perfect
Past Perfect
Future Perfect
Present Perfect Continuous
Past Perfect Continuous
Future Perfect Continuous
Irregular Verb Forms: Past Simple (V2)
Irregular Verb Forms: Past Participle (V3)
Irregular Verb Forms: Mixed
Full Conjugation / All Tenses

Any normal-training preset or manually composed configuration containing at least one premium tense/form must require Full Access.

Do not classify access based only on display labels or translated strings.

3. Important free default behavior

The free default training configuration must be:

Present Simple only

This applies when:

the app starts without a valid existing free configuration;
an old saved configuration points to premium content but the current user does not own Full Access;
the previous default Full Conjugation → All Tenses is no longer accessible to a free user;
a persisted premium normal-training session must be rejected.

Do NOT automatically create a mixed Present Simple + Past Simple + Future Simple session.

Do NOT automatically select all three free Simple tenses.

The user may manually choose Past Simple, Future Simple, or other available free presets from Training Mode.

The purpose of the fallback is only to provide a safe free default, not to redesign the user's training session.

4. Preset access must be semantic

Do not assume that a preset such as ps-all is free based on its ID or current name.

Inspect the actual configuration represented by each preset.

Access logic should be centralized through semantic helpers such as:

isPremiumPreset(...)

isPremiumSessionConfig(...)

canAccessTrainingPreset(...)

or equivalent.

No access decision may depend on:

visible labels;
translations;
UI text;
modal state;
button names.
5. Stable entitlement

Introduce a centralized semantic entitlement:

hasFullAccess

Keep purchase state, entitlement state, grammar access rules, and paywall UI separated.

Do not use a locally writable boolean as the authoritative proof of purchase.

A local cache may be used for UI/performance, but Play ownership must remain authoritative when available.

6. Google Play product

Use:

full_access

as the stable product ID.

This is:

one-time purchase;
non-consumable;
permanent Full Access;
not a subscription.

Never consume the product.

7. Purchase states

Support:

successful purchase;
pending purchase;
cancelled purchase;
failed purchase;
already-owned purchase;
restored purchase;
purchase completed after previously being pending.

A PENDING purchase must never grant Full Access.

During pending:

hasFullAccess remains false;
show an appropriate payment-processing state;
do not treat the purchase as completed;
do not acknowledge it as a completed entitlement yet.

Only grant Full Access after the store reports the purchase as successfully completed/purchased.

Then acknowledge the non-consumable purchase as required.

8. Purchase restoration

On native app launch:

initialize billing;
load full_access;
query/reconcile existing ownership;
update hasFullAccess;
listen for purchase-state changes.

Reconcile ownership again when the Android app returns to foreground.

The Restore action must trigger another explicit store refresh/query.

It must support:

app restart;
reinstall;
same Google account on another supported device;
purchase made before the current installation.
9. Training Mode UI

Premium presets must remain visible.

Add a clear lock indicator to premium items.

When a free user taps locked content:

do not change the active training configuration;
do not generate a premium exercise;
open the Full Access paywall.

Free Present Simple, Past Simple, and Future Simple remain selectable normally.

10. Mistakes Review exception

Mistakes Review remains free.

This includes an existing saved mistake whose original exercise used a premium tense or premium verb form.

A user must not be blocked from reviewing an already saved mistake merely because its original grammar content is now premium.

Preserve all:

mistakes;
progress;
statistics;
SRS data.
11. Persisted sessions

Validate restored practice_config and exercise_session before generating or restoring an exercise.

For a free user with a saved premium normal-training configuration:

do not restore or generate the premium exercise;
do not automatically open the sales paywall merely because the old saved state exists;
clear/ignore only the incompatible transient premium session state where necessary;
preserve statistics, progress, mistakes, SRS and other learning data;
switch the active normal-training configuration to Present Simple only.

Keep old persisted formats readable where reasonably possible for backward compatibility.

12. Settings

Add a prominent branded button using the app's existing primary blue/purple color.

Free state:

Открыть полный доступ

Owned state:

✓ Полный доступ открыт

The owned state should be green and non-promotional.

Tapping the owned state must not open the sales paywall.

13. Reusable paywall

Create one reusable Full Access paywall/modal.

Russian copy:

Полный доступ навсегда

Открой все времена английского языка, формы неправильных глаголов и режим полной тренировки.

[localized Google Play price] · один платёж

Primary button:

Открыть полный доступ — [localized price]

Secondary action:

Восстановить покупку

Include a normal close action.

Do not hardcode $4.99 in user-facing UI.

Use the localized price returned by Google Play Billing.

14. Automatic promotional paywall

Store scheduling data separately from purchase state.

Suggested persistent values:

full_access_first_use_at

full_access_last_auto_paywall_at

Runtime-only:

autoPaywallShownThisLaunch

Eligibility requires all of:

entitlement restoration has completed;
hasFullAccess === false;
onboarding is complete;
at least 48 hours have elapsed since first use;
at least 72 hours have elapsed since the last automatic promotional display;
it has not already been shown in the current app launch;
no paywall is already open.

On first use:

record first-use timestamp;
do not automatically show the paywall.

Therefore the promotional paywall must never appear on the user's first day.

Manual actions remain independent:

tapping locked premium content may always open the paywall;
tapping the Settings Full Access button may always open the paywall;
these manual opens do not need to obey the automatic promotional cooldown.
15. Browser/PWA

The browser/PWA version must handle unavailable native billing gracefully.

It must never grant production Full Access through:

a browser mock;
a local toggle;
manually edited storage.

Development mocks, if used, must be clearly separated from production entitlement logic.
