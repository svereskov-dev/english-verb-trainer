# Improve the "Past Tenses" Timeline

The current **Past** timeline needs a structural correction.

## Goal

The timeline should visually represent the chronological order of events from **earliest (left)** to **latest (right)**.

At the moment the tenses are arranged incorrectly.

---

## 1. Move Past Simple out of the timeline

Past Simple is the default past tense and should not be part of the timeline.

Instead, display it directly below the **Past** heading as a separate card.

Display:

### Past Simple

**Question**

> Что произошло?

**Formula**

> Subject + V2

**Example**

> I worked.

This card should appear **above** the timeline.

---

## 2. Reorder the timeline

After removing Past Simple, the timeline should contain only three tenses.

Arrange them from **left to right** in this order:

### 1. Past Perfect Continuous

**Question**

> Что происходило до другого события?

**Formula**

> Subject + had been + V-ing

**Example**

> I had been working.

---

### 2. Past Perfect

**Question**

> Что произошло раньше другого события?

**Formula**

> Subject + had + V3

**Example**

> I had worked.

---

### 3. Past Continuous

**Question**

> Что происходило в определенный момент?

**Formula**

> Subject + was/were + V-ing

**Example**

> I was working.

---

## 3. Timeline logic

The educational idea is simple:

* **Left** = earlier in time.
* **Right** = later in time.

Past Perfect Continuous represents the earliest action (a process that had already been happening).

Past Perfect represents an action completed before another past event.

Past Continuous represents an action in progress at a particular moment in the past.

The visual order should help learners understand the relationship between the tenses.
