### Training Mode – Make "Mistakes Review" Mutually Exclusive

The **Mistakes Review** option should behave exactly like **Full Conjugation → All Tenses**.

At the moment, **Mistakes Review** can be combined with other training filters. This creates confusing selection logic.

### Required behavior

Treat **Mistakes Review** as a standalone training mode.

It must **never** be active together with any other training options.

---

## Behavior

### 1. Selecting Mistakes Review

If the user clicks **Mistakes Review**:

* automatically deselect every other selected training option;
* activate **Mistakes Review** only.

After this action, **Mistakes Review** is the only active selection.

---

### 2. Selecting any other option while Mistakes Review is active

If **Mistakes Review** is already selected and the user clicks:

* any tense,
* Mixed,
* Full Conjugation,
* Regular/Irregular options,
* any other training filter,

then:

* automatically deselect **Mistakes Review**;
* activate the newly selected option(s) normally.

The user should never have to manually turn off Mistakes Review first.

---

## Logic

Mistakes Review is an independent training mode, not an additional filter.

Therefore it must be **mutually exclusive** with every other training mode.

It should behave exactly like **Full Conjugation**:

* selecting it clears everything else;
* selecting anything else automatically clears it.

---

## Validation Checklist

* Mistakes Review cannot remain active together with any other training option.
* Selecting Mistakes Review clears all existing selections.
* Selecting any other option automatically deselects Mistakes Review.
* No additional click is required.
* State persistence continues to work correctly.
* Reset Data restores the default behavior correctly.
