Critical Data Validation Task – Verify All Verbs and Verb Forms

We have identified a critical issue in the verb database.

Example:

listen
Past Simple: listenned ❌
Past Participle: listenned ❌

Correct forms are:

listen
listened
listened

This indicates that there may be additional data errors in the verb database.

Required task

Perform a complete audit of the entire verb database, not just the reported example.

Verify for every verb:

Base form (V1)
Past Simple (V2)
Past Participle (V3)
Verb type (Regular / Irregular)
IPA transcription (if stored)
Russian translation
Validation requirements

For regular verbs, verify that the generated forms follow English spelling rules correctly.

This includes, but is not limited to:

final -e
love → loved
live → lived
consonant + y
study → studied
carry → carried
vowel + y
play → played
enjoy → enjoyed
consonant doubling
stop → stopped
plan → planned
admit → admitted
verbs ending in c
panic → panicked
mimic → mimicked
verbs ending in l (British spelling rules, if intentionally used)
every other standard English spelling rule.

The current example (listen → listenned) suggests that the rule for consonant doubling is being applied too broadly.

Irregular verbs

Verify every irregular verb against a reliable English reference.

No incorrect V2 or V3 forms should remain.

Translation audit

Also verify that every Russian translation matches the English verb and does not contain accidental mismatches or duplicates.

IPA audit

Verify IPA transcriptions for consistency and correctness wherever they are stored.

Important

Do not fix only the reported examples.

Run a systematic validation across the entire database and correct every incorrect entry.

The goal is that the dictionary and the training engine use a 100% verified verb database, since users rely on it as a learning reference.

Deliverable

After completing the audit, provide a short report including:

total number of verbs checked;
number of issues found;
categories of issues (regular forms, irregular forms, translations, IPA, etc.);
confirmation that every issue has been corrected.
