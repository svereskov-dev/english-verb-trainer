Typography Refinement Across the Application

The application is now functionally complete, but the typography needs a final polish to create a more balanced and professional visual experience.

Please perform a typography review across the entire application and improve the visual hierarchy of text.

The goal is not to redesign the UI, but to make font sizes feel more balanced and consistent.

General principles

The interface should have a clear visual hierarchy:

the user's primary focus should still be the exercise itself;
secondary information should remain secondary;
no single text element should feel disproportionately large or unusually small.

Please review all major screens with this in mind.

Specific adjustments
Practice screen

The verb itself is currently too large compared to the rest of the interface.

Please reduce the verb font size slightly.

The change should be subtle—just enough that the layout feels more balanced while keeping the verb as the primary focal point.

Also slightly reduce the size of the placeholder text:

Type your answer...

It currently draws more attention than necessary.

The IPA transcription and translation do not need to match the verb size—they should remain visually secondary.

Dictionary list

On the Dictionary search page, the verb names are currently a little too small.

Please increase the font size slightly to improve readability while browsing.

Only a small increase is needed.

Dictionary verb detail page

On the individual verb page, the large verb title at the top feels oversized.

Please reduce it slightly so it remains prominent but feels more proportional to the rest of the page.

Review other screens

Please also review the typography on the remaining pages of the application.

If there are other places where font sizes feel visually inconsistent or out of proportion, make small adjustments to improve overall balance.

The objective is consistency—not redesign.

Important
Do not redesign layouts.
Do not change colors.
Do not change spacing unless necessary because of typography.
Do not alter the application's functionality.

The task is purely to improve the visual balance and consistency of typography throughout the application.

Goal

After this refinement, the application should feel more polished and cohesive. Users should immediately understand what deserves their attention first, second, and third, thanks to a consistent and well-balanced typography system.
