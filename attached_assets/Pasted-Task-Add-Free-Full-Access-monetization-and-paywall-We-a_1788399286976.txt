Task: Add Free / Full Access monetization and paywall

We are introducing a one-time Full Access purchase to the app.

The purchase is not a subscription. The user pays once and permanently unlocks all premium learning content.

1. Free content

These must remain fully available without purchase:

Present Simple
Past Simple
Future Simple
regular Practice mode
Context sentences
Letter Builder
Mistakes Review
statistics / progress / SRS
guides and other currently free educational content

Do not reduce the functionality of these free modes.

2. Premium content

Full Access unlocks:

Present Continuous
Past Continuous
Future Continuous
Present Perfect
Past Perfect
Future Perfect
Present Perfect Continuous
Past Perfect Continuous
Future Perfect Continuous
Irregular Verb Forms: Past Simple (V2)
Irregular Verb Forms: Past Participle (V3)
Irregular Verb Forms: Mixed
Full Conjugation / All Tenses

Any mixed preset that includes premium tenses must also require Full Access.

3. Stable entitlement

Introduce a semantic entitlement such as:

hasFullAccess

Do not determine premium access from button labels, translated strings, UI text, or local presentation state.

All premium checks should use the same centralized entitlement logic.

4. Training Mode

Show premium presets in the existing Training Mode screen, but visually mark them as locked.

Free Simple presets must remain selectable normally.

When a free user taps a locked preset:

do not start training;
open the Full Access paywall.

Do not completely hide premium presets. The user should be able to see what Full Access unlocks.

5. Settings

Add a prominent button to Settings using the app's existing primary blue/purple brand color.

Text for a free user:

Открыть полный доступ

Do not call it a subscription.

When Full Access is already owned, replace this with a green non-promotional state such as:

✓ Полный доступ открыт

The purchased state must not open the sales paywall.

6. Paywall

Create one reusable Full Access modal/screen.

Initial Russian copy:

Полный доступ навсегда

Открой все времена английского языка, формы неправильных глаголов и режим полной тренировки.

[localized Google Play price] · один платёж

Primary button:

Открыть полный доступ — [localized price]

Secondary action:

Восстановить покупку

Also provide a normal close button.

Do not hardcode $4.99 in UI strings.

Read and display the localized product price supplied by Google Play Billing.

7. Google Play purchase

Prepare the app for a non-consumable / one-time Google Play product representing permanent Full Access.

Use a stable product ID, for example:

full_access

The implementation must support:

purchase;
successful entitlement activation;
cancelled purchase;
failed purchase;
pending purchase;
restoring/re-querying an existing purchase;
app restart;
reinstall / same Google account where supported by Google Play purchase restoration.

Do not rely only on a locally stored boolean to prove that the purchase exists.

Local state may cache entitlement for UI/performance, but Google Play purchase state must be the source of truth when purchase information is available.

8. Automatic promotional paywall

Add a lightweight local scheduling mechanism for free users.

Requirements:

NEVER automatically show the promotional paywall on the first calendar day the app is used.
Record the first-use date.
The first automatic promotional paywall may appear only after at least 2 full days have passed since first use.
After that, show it no more often than once every 3 days.
Show it at most once per app launch.
Never show it to a user who already owns Full Access.
Store the timestamp/date of the last automatic paywall display.

Opening a premium feature manually is independent of this cooldown: tapping locked premium content should always be allowed to open the paywall.

9. Important UX rule

The user must be able to experience the complete training mechanics using the three free Simple tenses.

Context sentences, Letter Builder and Mistakes Review must NOT be paywalled.

We are monetizing additional grammar content, not the quality of the learning experience.

10. Architecture

Keep the implementation ready for localization.

Do not scatter checks such as:

if (preset.label === "...")

Prefer stable semantic fields / IDs and centralized helpers such as:

isPremiumPreset(...)

hasFullAccess

canAccessTrainingPreset(...)

Keep purchase state, paywall UI, and grammar-content access logic separated.

11. Before changing code

First inspect the current project and report:

which files/components will need to change;
whether Google Play Billing / Capacitor purchase support already exists;
which package/plugin you propose using for Android purchases;
how entitlement restoration will work;
how you will prevent the automatic paywall from appearing on the first day;
whether any current persisted session could reference a newly premium preset and what should happen to such a session for a free user.

Do not implement anything yet.

Wait for approval after this audit.
