I want to improve Context Mode.

Currently, Context exercises display only a generic label:

FILL THE GAP

This forces users to do two different tasks at once:

1. Determine which tense the sentence requires.
2. Recall the correct verb form.

The primary purpose of this application is to train English verb forms, not tense recognition. Context mode should provide context for the verb, but the target tense should be explicit.

Required change:

Replace the generic "FILL THE GAP" label with the actual tense name used by the exercise.

Examples:

Current:

FILL THE GAP

At last, he _____ everything on the list.
(pay)

Desired:

PAST PERFECT

At last, he _____ everything on the list.
(pay)

Another example:

Current:

FILL THE GAP

By the end of the month, you _____ everything at least once.
(set)

Desired:

FUTURE PERFECT

By the end of the month, you _____ everything at least once.
(set)

Implementation requirements:

- Remove the generic "FILL THE GAP" heading.
- Display the exercise tense instead.
- Use the same visual style already used for tense labels in Full Conjugation mode.
- The label should be generated directly from exercise.tense.
- Convert internal tense keys into user-friendly labels:
  
  presentSimple → PRESENT SIMPLE
  pastSimple → PAST SIMPLE
  futureSimple → FUTURE SIMPLE
  
  presentContinuous → PRESENT CONTINUOUS
  pastContinuous → PAST CONTINUOUS
  futureContinuous → FUTURE CONTINUOUS
  
  presentPerfect → PRESENT PERFECT
  pastPerfect → PAST PERFECT
  futurePerfect → FUTURE PERFECT
  
  presentPerfectContinuous → PRESENT PERFECT CONTINUOUS
  pastPerfectContinuous → PAST PERFECT CONTINUOUS
  futurePerfectContinuous → FUTURE PERFECT CONTINUOUS
  
  presentSimplePassive → PRESENT SIMPLE PASSIVE
  pastSimplePassive → PAST SIMPLE PASSIVE
  presentPerfectPassive → PRESENT PERFECT PASSIVE
  pastPerfectPassive → PAST PERFECT PASSIVE
  futureSimplePassive → FUTURE SIMPLE PASSIVE

- Keep the sentence, verb hint, Russian translation, answer field, validation logic, and all other UI elements unchanged.

Goal:

Context Mode should train verb forms within context, not the user's ability to guess the target tense. The required tense must always be visible to the learner.
